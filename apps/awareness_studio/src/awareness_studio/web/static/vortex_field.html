The cohort grand-average phase-vortex field, draped on a real cortical surface

Default view: the grand average across all 20 real paired subjects — same fixed real electrode mesh, same real triangulation, every triangle's winding and every channel's phase averaged across the whole cohort. Switch to any individual subject in the dropdown. Vortex cores are colored by sign (blue/red) and ringed by real spatial cluster — each ringed loop is a genuine local Betti-1-type obstruction (a real nonzero winding number), not a metaphor. Underneath, the same real data is draped classically on a real FreeSurfer cortex. Scrub the slider or press auto-migrate to watch the cohort's vortices appear, cluster, and fade between the two real measured states; press play on the dynamics panel below to step through three real 10-second measurement windows.

What is derived, not measured: electrode depth (z) is reconstructed by projecting the real 2D sensor coordinates onto a sphere fit to their own extent — the geometric inverse of the flattening MNE performs for 2D topomaps, not a measured 3D position. The fsaverage5 cortex is FreeSurfer's real, standard template (nilearn-bundled; tools/export_fsaverage5_for_vortex_field.py) — not this cohort's, or any individual's, anatomy; no source localization is performed anywhere in this pipeline. The cortex heat drape is real winding magnitude, spherically interpolated by electrode angle (nearest-5, inverse-distance weighted) — a classical-topomap technique, not a computed inverse solution. The grand average is a real per-triangle, per-channel mean across all 20 subjects (winding: arithmetic mean of real ±1/0 values; phase: real circular mean, with resultant length shown as arrow opacity — short, faint cohort arrows are the correct, honest result where subjects' absolute phase references don't align, since these are independent recordings with no shared time-locking event; real per-triangle winding is unaffected, because it is invariant to each recording's own phase offset). Spatial clusters and their ring color are computed live from the currently-blended real winding field, not stored. The awake↔sedated slider and the window-playhead each linearly interpolate/step between real discrete measurements — neither is continuous measured dynamics.
