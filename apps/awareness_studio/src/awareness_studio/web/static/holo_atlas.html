One brain, every real signal, one animation

A single semi-transparent volumetric scene combining every real visualization this repository produces: the EEG phase-vortex field draped on the cortex with skyrmion-style vortex cores and real Betti-1 loop rings, the ketamine fMRI connectome as a real, weighted node-edge structure fit inside the cortical volume, and the Mapper cohort-structure network orbiting alongside as a satellite graph. Every surface uses one continuous low→high energy spectrum (deep blue → cyan → green → yellow → red) so activity intensity reads identically across all three real datasets. Press Play all to watch every dataset animate through its own real recorded states at once.

What is real vs. illustrative placement — read before interpreting node positions:
  • EEG scalp mesh + cortex heat: real ds005620 electrode positions, triangulation, winding numbers, and phase, spherically interpolated onto FreeSurfer's real fsaverage5 template surface (a standard group-average anatomy, not this cohort's own scan).
  • Interior connectome: one real ds005917 subject's real 100-parcel KMeans centroids (native in-brain voxel space, so their relative geometry is real brain-shaped structure) and real Pearson connectivity, uniformly rescaled to fit inside this fsaverage5 mesh. This is an illustrative fit, not a co-registration — a different subject, different modality, different scanner space than the EEG cortex above.
  • Mapper satellite graph: real KeplerMapper nodes/edges over real per-window cohort features; its 3D layout is a force-directed spring simulation computed for display only — Mapper nodes carry no spatial/anatomical coordinate at all.
  • Combined animation: the EEG awake↔sedated and fMRI drug↔placebo timelines are two independent real experiments on different subjects, driven together here for one continuous visual narrative — not a claim they were recorded simultaneously.
